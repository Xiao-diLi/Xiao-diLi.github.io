<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

I am a researcher working on **quantum computation** and **quantum information theory**.

During my Ph.D. at Zhejiang University (2016–2021), I focused on theoretical high-energy physics, particularly on **scattering amplitudes** and **Feynman integral reduction**, developing new algebraic and differential-operator methods for computing one-loop integrals and expanding Einstein-Yang-Mills amplitudes. After graduating, I continued this line of research as a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

In 2022, I joined [Turing Quantum](https://www.turingq.com/) (Shanghai) as a quantum algorithm engineer, where I gained hands-on experience bridging quantum theory with practical algorithm development. Since 2023, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at Fudan University, where my work has shifted toward **quantum information theory** — including quantum state verification via classical shadows, entanglement certification, quantum information masking, and algorithms for neutral-atom array assembly.

## Research Interests

- Quantum state verification and certification
- Quantum entanglement and quantum correlations
- Quantum error correction
- Neutral-atom quantum computing
- Scattering amplitudes and Feynman integrals
